Common Questions

Everything you
need to know

Do I need technical knowledge to work with StratAI? +
No. Our entire process is designed for business owners and executives. You speak your answers — we handle all technical implementation. If you can describe your problem in plain English, we can solve it.
What is included in the pricing — are tool costs extra? +
Yes — clearly. All StratAI prices are our consulting and build fees only. Platform subscriptions like Make.com, Zapier, HubSpot, Claude API, and app platforms are paid directly by you to those providers. We itemise every expected tool cost in your proposal so there are no surprises. For reference, most SMB clients spend AED 330–1,285/month on platforms after we set everything up.
How quickly will I see results? +
The first automation or dashboard is live within 21 days of engagement start. This is deliberate — quick wins demonstrate ROI early, build internal confidence, and create momentum for the broader programme.
Our data is messy. Can we still implement AI? +
Yes — and this is one of the most common situations we encounter. Our Data Health Check diagnoses exactly what needs remediation. We prioritise quick wins that can proceed even with imperfect data while fixing the foundation in parallel. Messy data doesn't stop us — it becomes part of what we fix.
Do you understand UAE regulations and PDPL? +
Yes. All governance frameworks include UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) compliance considerations. We are based in Dubai and incorporate PDPL into every data strategy and governance deliverable as a standard requirement.
What legal agreements will I need to sign? +
Four: an NDA (before any confidential information is shared), a Data Processing Agreement (before any data flows through our systems), a Master Service Agreement (overall terms), and a Statement of Work for each project. All signed digitally via PandaDoc. Templates reviewed for UAE law.
What happens after the engagement ends? +
All clients receive a dedicated Notion portal with every deliverable, dashboard, and document. You own everything we build — it's in your accounts, not ours. Monthly retainers are available for ongoing support and evolution as your business grows.
